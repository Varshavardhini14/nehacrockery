OUR STORY

Three Decades of
Glassware Excellence

Welcome to Neha Crockery House, Chennai's premier glassware wholesaler. For over 30 years, we have been the go-to destination for glassware enthusiasts in Tamil Nadu.

From elegant wine glasses to stylish tumblers, our extensive collection is perfect for both personal and business needs. We have built lasting relationships with clients across the region by delivering exceptional quality and dependable service.

Our showroom, located in the heart of Chennai at Rattan Bazaar, Park Town, welcomes wholesale buyers, retailers, and hospitality businesses seeking the finest glassware selections.
