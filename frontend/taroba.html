OUR STORY

The Taroba
Brand Story

In 2016, Neha Crockery House took a landmark step — filing for the official trademark registration of Taroba: our very own in-house brand of kitchen and tableware.

After over two decades of sourcing and supplying other brands to businesses across Tamil Nadu, we created Taroba to offer our customers a trusted, homegrown alternative — products we stand behind completely because we control the quality and sourcing ourselves.

Taroba covers four core categories that represent the heart of our business: crockery & glassware, ceramic products, flasks and cutleries. Every Taroba product meets the same standards we've maintained at Neha Crockery House for over 35 years.
